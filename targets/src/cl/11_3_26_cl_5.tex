\noindent Dear SpaceX Hiring Team,

I am writing to apply for the \textbf{Software Engineer, Full Stack (Data \& Video Analysis)} position on
the Vehicle Analysis Tooling team. For the past five years at Electronic Arts, I have built internal tools,
data pipelines, and performance-critical systems for the people who depend on them every day. I am drawn
to this role because it asks an engineer to own the full path from processing to interface to operations,
and to learn unfamiliar parts quickly enough to ship.

In my current role at \textbf{EA Lifestyle}, I architected an extensible Maya/Python animation stylization
tool with PySide/Qt interfaces that was adopted across multiple internal studios and reduced redundant
manual keyframing by over 70\%. I also prototyped and integrated ML-assisted pipelines using PyTorch and
ONNX Runtime, adding automated asset tagging and procedural pose generation while leaving artists in
control of the result. Separately, I engineered a high-throughput Unreal Engine C++ plugin that exports
runtime character trajectory, pose, and velocity data to train and populate motion-matching databases.

At \textbf{EA Maxis Studios}, I worked on \emph{Project Rene} (\emph{The Sims}), where I learned to treat
performance as something users feel directly. I architected a multi-threaded, asynchronous path-planning
API in UE5/C++ that held to a frame budget under 1.5ms, and I profiled runtime memory and frame budgets to
eliminate hitches during dense crowd simulation. I also partnered directly with technical animators and
gameplay designers to build in-editor tuning tools and visual debuggers, shaping them around the problems
those teams were trying to solve.

My full-stack and Python work extends beyond the studio. I built \emph{JAMN Sales Tracker}, a Python/Flask
application that aggregates records from Square, Squarespace, Venmo, and Google Sheets, then standardizes
and categorizes them into net-earnings reports. During my internship at \emph{TurnUp}, I developed a React
Native app and Express.js REST API while leading an intern team, handling code reviews, and writing unit
tests. On the LLM side, I use tools such as Claude Code and Codex daily and built an agentic pipeline that
reads a document, generates LaTeX, compiles it, and checks the output. I am also completing graduate
coursework in machine learning and deep learning through the University of Washington.

My background is in games, so engineering video and flight telemetry would be new domains for me. What I
would bring is depth in data-heavy software, applied models, and tools that engineers and artists rely on,
along with a record of working closely with specialists on their hardest problems. I would welcome the chance
to apply that experience to the tools behind production, test, and flight at SpaceX. Thank you for your
time and consideration.
