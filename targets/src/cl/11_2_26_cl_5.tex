At \textbf{EA Maxis Studios}, I owned engine-level systems in Unreal Engine 5. I architected a multi-threaded,

obstacle avoidance within a frame budget of under 1.5ms, and implemented core locomotion, character physics,
event systems, and runtime animation, including motion matching, distance matching, and custom IK solvers
built on 3D math and linear algebra. I profiled, benchmarked, and optimized CPU, memory, and frame budgets
across character evaluation graphs and spatial queries to eliminate hitches in dense simulations, and partnered
with designers, technical animators, and QA to build in-editor tuning tools and visual debuggers.

At \textbf{EA Lifestyle}, I architected a project-agnostic Maya/Python stylization tool adopted across multiple
internal studios that cut redundant keyframing work by over 70\%, which taught me how to build shared technology
that balances the needs of different productions. I also engineered a high-throughput Unreal Engine C++ plugin
that captures runtime character data for motion-matching databases, and integrated ML-assisted authoring
(PyTorch, ONNX) into DCC tools while keeping artists fully in control of the output.

I take ongoing AI skill development seriously. I use Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex daily, and I built an
agentic pipeline that orchestrates coding agents with authored skills and scoped context, enforcing
truthfulness rules and verifying every output, which is my own practice of the quality and accuracy reviews
responsible AI requires. Outside of work, I also built a custom C++ engine (\emph{Hoowan Game Engine}) with an
OpenGL renderer, input and event handling, collision, an entity-component system, and a desktop editor.

Based in Seattle, I would be glad to bring this experience to Reality Labs and help keep the engine fast,
reliable, and delightful for the developers who build on it. Thank you for your time and consideration. I look
forward to speaking with you.
